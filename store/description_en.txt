[Title]
zzHeartPlus - Heart Rate, Zone, Training Type

[Description]
A data field that shows your current heart rate and zone in big digits, and draws a histogram of the time spent in each zone while you record.
In large wide fields it also analyzes your heart rate distribution so far and shows what kind of session you are riding (training intensity distribution, TID).
Built with the TitilliumWeb font for better readability on the move.
A rebuilt version of zzHeartChart.

- Needs a heart rate sensor (chest strap or optical). No phone connection required.

[Heart rate and zone]
- Current heart rate in big digits that fill the field.
- Your current zone next to the heart, in the zone color. Show it as a decimal like 2.4 (position inside the zone) or as a whole number (setting).
- Average, max or lap average heart rate in the top right corner (or none).
- An arrow on the zone color bar at the bottom points to your current heart rate.

[Time in zone histogram]
- Time is collected per zone while the timer runs, and each bar shows that zone's share (%) of the total recorded time.
- Paused and auto-paused time is left out.
- Large wide fields show the percentage above each bar.
- Bars can be striped (keeps the digits easy to read) or solid.
- If the app restarts during a ride, the histogram carries on.

[Training type analysis]
- In large wide fields (1 or 2 field layouts), heart rate and the second value go in the top half and the histogram in the bottom half.
- After 10 minutes of recording, your time-in-zone distribution is compared with 8 TID models and the closest one is shown right under your heart rate.
  Example: HVLI(92%) - High Volume Low Intensity
- The number in brackets is how much your distribution overlaps the model's (%).
- The 8 models: HVLI, HIT, PRMD (pyramidal), R.PRMD (reverse pyramidal), THRD (threshold), POL (polarized), R.POL (reverse polarized), EVEN
- The analysis only runs every 10 seconds in large wide fields, so other fields use no extra battery. It can be turned off in settings.

[Colors]
- Current heart rate changes color when it is a set percentage above or below the second value (average etc.). Pick separate colors for above and below.
- Choose "Heart zone color" to always show your heart rate in its zone color.
- The background follows your device setting (white or black).

[Heart rate zones]
- By default the zones set on your device are used (current sport profile).
- Or calculate from max heart rate: Default, Joe Friel, Andrew Coggan, USA Cycling, British Cycling, Strava, Garmin
- Or enter zones 1 to 5 yourself.

[Layouts, picked automatically]
- One slot of the 10-field layout: heart, zone and second value on top, large current heart rate below.
- Wide slots: zzHeartPlus title with the active value (AVG / MAX / LAP) on top, both values side by side at the same size below.
- Large wide slots: the training type analysis screen (or the wide layout if turned off).
- Same typeface, sizes and layout as zzSpeedPlus and zzCadencePlus, so they look consistent side by side.

[Recording]
- Your current zone is saved in the activity, so you can see it as a chart in Garmin Connect.
- Time in each zone (%) is saved in the activity summary.

[Supported devices]
Edge 540 / 550 / 840 / 850 / 1040 / 1050 / 1060

Change settings in the Garmin Connect IQ app or Garmin Express.
